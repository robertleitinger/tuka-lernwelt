🙋‍♀️About me

Hi, I'm Lea. I'm ten years old. I live in Puch bei Hallein. I've got blue eyes and long, blond hair. My school is in Salzburg. I'm in class 1C. I haven't got a brother, but I've got a sister. I'm tall and I've got a hamster. His name is Lu. I like riding horses. My favourite colours are blue, purple and light green. I love painting and playing games at home.

👭My best friend

My best friend's name is Mona. She is from Puch bei Hallein in Austria. She is ten years old. Mona has got blond hair and green eyes. She is small. She has got a sister, but she hasn't got a brother. Her sister's name is Alma. Mona is nice and pretty. She likes swimming, reading books and playing games.

🏫My classroom

My classroom is 1C. There is a blackboard on the white wall. There are twenty-six brown tables and twenty-six brown chairs. There are two doors and they are white. The floor is light brown and there is a computer on the teacher's desk. There are nine windows on the left. Frederik-Günter is next to the windows.

🏴‍☠️The pirate Frank

This is the pirate Frank. He is tall and he has got a tattoo on his right arm. He has got an orange beard and a red hairband. There is a grey cat on his shoulder. Frank has got a brown shirt and brown trousers. He has got a wooden left leg, but he hasn't got a hook.

😊My week

I've got football on Monday and I'm tired. I've got an English test on Tuesday and I'm nervous. I've got tennis on Wednesday and I'm hot. I'm excited because there is music club on Thursday. I'm happy because I go to the cinema with Elias on Friday.
