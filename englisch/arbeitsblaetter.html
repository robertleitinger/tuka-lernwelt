Mehrzahl: meistens -s (dog - dogs). Mitlaut + y wird -ies (baby - babies). Selbstlaut + y: nur -s (boy - boys). Ausnahmen: child - children, fish - fish.

Befehl: Das Verb steht am Anfang: Open the door! Verneint mit Don't: Don't speak!

1Schreib das Zahlwort und die Mehrzahl in die Lücke.

  3. There are twenty fish in the water. (20, fish)
  4. Fifteen boys are at the zoo. (15, boy)
  5. There are two families in the wildlife park. (2, family)

Unit 3: Pirates

Grammatik zum Ausfüllen · have got / has got · Fragen · Mehrzahl · Lösungen

Name: Datum:
Merk dir

I / you / we / they have got (I've got). He / she / it has got (he's got). Verneint: haven't got / hasn't got.

Frage: Have you got …? Has she got …? Kurzantwort: Yes, I have. / No, she hasn't. Mehrzahl: foot - feet, tooth - teeth.

1Schau auf ✓ oder ✗. Setz have got, has got, haven't got oder hasn't got ein.

  1. I have got a parrot. (✓)
  2. My brother has got blue eyes. (✓)
  3. Tom hasn't got a hat. (✗)
  4. The pirates have got a big ship. (✓)
  5. We haven't got a dog. (✗)
  6. The captain hasn't got a hook. (✗)

2Ergänze die Fragen und die Kurzantworten.

  1. Have you got a dog? (✓) Yes, I have (we have).
  2. Has Mia got a brother? (✗) No, she hasn't.
  3. Have they got a ship? (✓) Yes, they have.
  4. Has the pirate got a beard? (✓) Yes, he has.
  5. Has your cat got long hair? (✗) No, it hasn't (he hasn't, she hasn't).
  6. Have you and Tom got a cat? (✓) Yes, we have.

3Setz die Mehrzahl ein.

  1. The pirate has got big feet. (foot)
  2. The crocodile has got big teeth. (tooth)
  3. My cat has got green eyes. (eye)
  4. Three children are on the ship. (child)
  5. The bear has got four legs. (leg)
  6. There are five fish in the sea. (fish)

4Schau dir die Bilder an und ergänze die Sprechblasen.

Unit 4: Emotions

Grammatik zum Ausfüllen · to be verneint · Fragen und Kurzantworten · Lösungen

Name: Datum:
Merk dir

Verneint: I'm not · you aren't · he / she / it isn't · we / you / they aren't.

Frage: Are you happy? Is he cold? Am I right? Kurzantwort: Yes, I am. / No, he isn't. / No, you aren't.

1Setz 'm not, isn't oder aren't ein.

'm notisn'taren't
  1. I 'm not (am not) tired.
  2. Mike isn't happy.
  3. We aren't late.
  4. The tourists aren't bored.
  5. It isn't Monday today.
  6. You aren't wrong.

2Ergänze die Fragen und die Kurzantworten.

  1. Are you hungry? (✓) Yes, I am (we are).
  2. Is Lisa scared? (✗) No, she isn't.
  3. Are they from Spain? (✗) No, they aren't.
  4. Is it Friday today? (✓) Yes, it is.
  5. Am I right? (✗) No, you aren't.
  6. Are Mia and Ben excited? (✓) Yes, they are.

3Ergänze das Gespräch mit der richtigen Form von to be.

Mia

Leo
Happy birthday, Mia! Is your party today?
Mia
No, it isn't (is not). It's on Saturday.
